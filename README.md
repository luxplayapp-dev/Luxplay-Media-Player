Privacy Policy for LuxPlay

LuxPlay is a multimedia player application that allows users to play video and audio files stored on their device.

1. Data Collection
LuxPlay does not collect, store, or share any personal data from users.

2. Permissions Usage
LuxPlay may request the following permissions:
- Media (Video/Audio/Images): To scan and display your local media files.
- Microphone: Used only for voice search or screen recording features.
- Notifications: To provide background playback controls.
- Storage (for older Android versions): To access local files.

All data remains on your device. LuxPlay does not upload any data to external servers.

3. Third-Party Services
LuxPlay does not use third-party analytics or advertising services.

4. Data Sharing
We do not share any user data with third parties.

5. Security
All operations are performed locally on your device.

6. Changes
This policy may be updated in future versions.

7. Contact
For any queries, contact: luxplay.app@gmail.com
